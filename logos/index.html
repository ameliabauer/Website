A human + AI creative experiment

Logo Playground

These concepts began with AI generating a range of visual directions. My part was to bring the human judgement: shaping the brief, reacting to the ideas, giving feedback and steering the iterations. The result is a playful collection of possibilities — a little reminder that AI can generate, but the creative direction is still a collaboration.
